About Mech3DLab

Practical ideas transformed into printable solutions.

The models presented here are designed with usability, printability and durability in mind. Each design begins with a practical need and is developed into a model that can be produced on a common FDM 3D printer.

Purpose-driven

Models are created to solve practical problems in the workshop, garage, on a motorcycle or at home.

Designed for printing

Print orientation, strength, assembly and material usage are considered during the design process.

Tested and refined

Prototypes are printed and checked before the final model is prepared for publication.
