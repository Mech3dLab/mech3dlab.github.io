My story

Practical design based on real needs.

My name is David and I create functional 3D printable models under the Mech3DLab name. I focus primarily on technical and practical products for workshops, motorcycles, organization and everyday use.

Most designs begin with a specific problem or a missing product. Instead of creating decorative objects, I prefer to develop parts that have a clear purpose and can be used in real situations.

My goal is to create models that are functional, understandable and suitable for printing on commonly available FDM 3D printers.

This is an initial version of the About Me text. It can later be expanded with information about your background, equipment, CAD software and printers.

Design process

How a model is created

The process takes a model from an initial practical need to a printable digital product.

  1. Identify the problem A practical need, missing accessory or opportunity for a better solution is identified.
  2. Measure and plan Important dimensions, limitations, connections and material requirements are considered.
  3. Create the CAD model The design is developed with attention to function, manufacturing and ease of printing.
  4. Print the prototype A test version is printed to check fit, strength and real-world usability.
  5. Test and refine The design is adjusted according to the results of practical testing.
  6. Publish the final model The finished model is prepared with appropriate information and offered through Cults3D.
