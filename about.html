My approach

I transform mechanical ideas into printable products.

My name is David and I create functional 3D printable models under the Mech3DLab name. My work is influenced by mechanical engineering, workshops, engines and practical problem solving.

I prefer products with a clear purpose, recognizable technical character and enjoyable assembly process. Each design is developed with real printing and use in mind.

From idea to model

Design process

  1. Identify the idea or problem
    A practical requirement or mechanical concept defines the starting point.
  2. Measure and plan
    Dimensions, movement, assembly, material and print limitations are considered.
  3. Create the CAD model
    The concept is transformed into printable components.
  4. Prototype and test
    Test prints verify fit, strength, function and appearance.
  5. Refine and publish
    The final model is prepared for Cults3D.
